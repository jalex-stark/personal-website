Help us make these games better.

Peel, Little Trips, and Switchyard automatically send gameplay action logs to this site's private playtest archive. We use them to understand confusing controls, failed moves, puzzle difficulty, and what people enjoy.

Logs include board states, moves, selections, rotations, hints, undo, game control keys, clicks, drag paths, timing, errors, puzzle results, and notes you submit. A random browser ID links your visits; each page visit has its own session ID. We don't record text as you type in feedback fields, or store your IP address or browser user-agent in the action archive.

The archive is private and stored on Netlify. We may use coding agents to analyze it. Avoid including personal information in submitted notes. Your progress still saves in your browser.

Unsent actions wait in browser storage and retry when you return or reconnect. You can also export action logs from the games. The archive currently has no automatic expiration.
